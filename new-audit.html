Method, evidence quality and limitations

Review date: 27 October 2026. The analysis combines (a) live PageSpeed Insights runs on all three sites, mobile and desktop, (b) the Google Analytics 4 snapshot for Palisson, (c) the Search Console workbook for NMKCA, (d) the public service architecture and page source of cybit.com, and (e) Google's published guidance on Core Web Vitals and AI search.

Evidence tierWhat it supportsLimits
PageSpeed Insights runs CLS, LCP, TBT and TTFB ratings for all three sites, mobile and desktop. Lab data. Field data (CrUX) may differ, particularly on mobile. Treat the ratings as directional rather than definitive.
Direct uploaded evidence Palisson GA4 snapshot, NMKCA Search Console export and filters. GA4 PDF is a screenshot without event definitions or page-path drill-down. GSC workbook covers only the dimensions and rows included.
Public-page observation Cybit service page structure and CTAs. NMKCA and Palisson homepage text and counters. No CMS, server logs, private analytics, CRM, user testing, crawl export or source-code audit was available. Crawler-extracted text can differ from fully rendered browser state.
Recommendations / hypotheses Prioritised next tests and fixes based on the evidence and role brief. Proposed actions, not claims that an unverified defect exists. Validate before implementation.

Core Web Vitals thresholds used for evaluation

At the 75th percentile, "good" targets are LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms and CLS ≤ 0.1. These are evaluation targets. The measured values for Cybit and NMKCA (CLS ≈ 0.9) sit outside the "good" band by a factor of nine.

Google's guidance on AI search

Google's own guidance says the established SEO fundamentals remain relevant for AI search features, and that there are no additional technical requirements or special AI-specific schema needed for eligibility. The recommendations here therefore focus on crawlability, helpful content, clear text, internal linking, evidence, and structured data that accurately matches visible content.

Scope note

This report distinguishes source evidence, direct page observations and hypotheses requiring validation. It is a working interview document. It does not claim access to the three organisations' CMS, private analytics properties, CRM or server logs. All PageSpeed readings are lab runs taken on 27 October 2026 and should be re-run before quoting as final.

Pressure touchpoints and proposed improvements

P0CLS is a conversion leak

A CLS of ~0.9 on desktop, worse on mobile, suppresses form completion and webinar registration. Every layout shift is a moment where a prospect loses their place mid-form or mid-CTA.

Fix: trace the shift source in the PageSpeed filmstrip. Reserve space for HubSpot forms and banners. Set explicit dimensions on hero media. Defer non-critical third-party tags until after interaction.

Measure: CLS ≤ 0.1. Form completion rate. Webinar registration rate. Field data once sample size allows.

P0Measurement to CRM

No Cybit GA4, GSC or CRM baseline was available for this audit. This is a data gap, not proof that tracking is absent. But it means the current form-to-pipeline path cannot be verified from outside.

Fix: audit GA4 events, consent state, HubSpot and CRM properties, Lead Forensics, Sales Navigator and webinar hand-offs. Map every form, assessment booking, phone and email click, gated asset and webinar registration.

Measure: event QA pass rate. Lead-source completeness. Form-submit-to-CRM match rate. Visitor-to-lead conversion. Marketing-sourced and influenced pipeline.

P1Third-party script governance

Four tracking platforms load on the homepage. Each competes for main-thread time and each contributes to layout shift unless explicitly controlled.

Fix: load on interaction or after consent. Set explicit container dimensions. Audit which tags fire before the first paint.

Measure: TBT, INP, consent rate, and CLS stability during releases.

P1Landing page conversion architecture

Service pages are content-rich, but buyers arriving with a specific problem may not find a crisp next step or relevant proof quickly enough.

Experiment: for each priority page, test a clear primary CTA, a lower-commitment secondary CTA, one relevant case study or outcome, scope and fit qualifiers, and a concise "what happens next" section.

Measure: CTA click-to-submit. Form completion. Assessment booking rate. Qualified-lead rate by page.

P1Information architecture

Multiple corporate and content destinations (service pages, technologies, blog, Press Centre, Engage Hub, On Demand, Store). That breadth is an asset but can complicate choice if paths are not clearly tiered.

Fix: use GA4 path exploration and heatmaps to confirm whether target buyers find the right service route. Add prominent "I need to…" pathways and contextual links between problem, service, case study and CTA.

Measure: path progression. Service-to-case-study CTR. Exits at key pages. Contact-page assist rate.

P1AI visibility

The role explicitly expects visibility in ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews. No AI visibility baseline exists for Cybit.

Fix: create a repeatable prompt set by buyer, vertical and solution. Record whether Cybit is mentioned or cited, which competitors appear, which source pages are cited and what content gaps recur.

Measure: share of tracked prompts with a correct mention. Citation frequency. Cited page set. AI-assisted visits and leads.

P1Non-brand discoverability

No Cybit Search Console export or ranking data was available. Query gaps cannot be quantified without it.

Fix: build a baseline around problem and solution searches for CIO, IT, CISO and data leaders. Map each intent cluster to one strong landing page and useful supporting content.

Measure: non-brand clicks and impressions. Qualified organic leads. Priority-query coverage. Assisted pipeline.

P2Structured data depth

Yoast Premium outputs a basic schema graph, but Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Review markup on service and campaign pages is likely incomplete.

Fix: deploy Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Review schema where eligible. Validate against visible content before publishing.

Measure: rich result impressions. CTR on pages with structured data.

The best next move is optimisation, not a wholesale redesign. The content foundation is strong and the conversion infrastructure exists. Use behavioural evidence to determine which entry pages fail to progress visitors, and test small changes against a control.

Pressure touchpoints and recommended fixes

P0Suspicious URL patterns

Search Console contains paths that do not fit an accounting website: "?goods/…", "?jp/…", "/outlet/…" and "/ctg/search/similarImageSearchResultView/".

Action: inspect exact URLs in a browser and Search Console URL Inspection. Check response, canonical, indexability, sitemap, CMS records, plugins and users, and server and security logs. If confirmed invalid, clean up the source and return appropriate 404, 410 or redirects. Do not mass-redirect unrelated junk without validation.

Measure: no unapproved spam URLs in the index. Clean sitemap. Stable indexed-page count. No recurring suspicious URL patterns.

P0CLS is the same leak as Cybit

Desktop CLS is around 0.9. Mobile is worse. The site is unstable on load, which affects both engagement and search quality signals. WordPress with unmanaged script loading is a common cause.

Action: trace the shift source, reserve space for dynamic elements, defer non-critical scripts, set explicit media dimensions.

Measure: CLS ≤ 0.1. Engagement time. Contact form completion.

P0Zero-value trust counters

Homepage text extraction shows multiple trust counters at 0, while the page copy separately states 24+ years of industry experience. Crawlers and assistive technology may see zeroes where the browser eventually shows numbers.

Action: check the rendered page, count-up animation and server-rendered text. Make key proof points meaningful without JavaScript, and ensure counts are accurate.

Measure: consistent counters across browser, accessibility tree and crawler extraction. No zero-value trust metrics.

P1Legacy PDF ranking

"NMK-Company-Profile-2017-emailer.pdf": 676 impressions, 14 clicks, 2.07% CTR, position 6.45. A nine-year-old PDF is the second-best performing asset on the site.

Action: confirm whether the profile is current. Replace with a current branded PDF and accurate metadata. Update internal links and decide whether the legacy URL should be retained, redirected or retired based on link equity and user need.

Measure: current asset receives clicks and downloads. Updated dates, service information and contact details. No stale company claims.

P1Homepage concentration

The homepage is the main indexed page (223 clicks, 5,188 impressions, 4.3% CTR, position 8.75). Service query intent is dispersed.

Action: map each high-value service to a dedicated page with descriptive title and H1, clear scope, South African context, proof, FAQs and a single primary CTA. Link service, industry, about, leadership and contact pages logically.

Measure: non-brand clicks. High-intent landing-page visits. Contact and consultation conversion by service.

P1Mobile CTR gap

Mobile has lower CTR than desktop (3.82% versus 5.36%) even though average position is stronger.

Action: check mobile SERP appearance and run a device-specific journey audit covering viewport, speed, menu, text scale, tap targets, contact and WhatsApp link, and form friction. Segment by query before attributing cause.

Measure: mobile CTR and mobile lead conversion by landing page. Fewer form drop-offs.

P2No meaningful rich-result signal

Search appearance rows show one impression each for review and product snippets across six months.

Action: validate structured data against visible content — Organization, AccountingService or LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList, Person where relevant. Use FAQ content to answer users, not to chase guaranteed rich snippets.

Measure: valid structured data where eligible. Stronger SERP clarity. Qualified organic traffic.

Pressure touchpoints and recommended fixes

P0Key-event configuration is missing

3.4k events are recorded, but the key-event card shows no data. Events are being captured; conversions are not being defined.

Action: define and test key events — contact or brief submit, WhatsApp click, phone and email click, book-a-call, portfolio and case-study engagement, downloads, newsletter submit. Mark only meaningful outcomes as key events.

Measure: QA pass for every key event. Lead events visible in GA4 and matched to inbox or CRM.

P0Zero-value trust counters

Public page text extraction shows "0+" for Projects Completed, Years of Experience and Satisfied Clients. The visual page may animate these values after load, but crawlers and assistive technology see zeros.

Action: ensure static, semantically readable values render to crawlers and assistive technology. If metrics are not verified, replace counts with factual proof statements rather than animated zeroes.

Measure: consistent counters in rendered browser, crawl and accessibility review. Fewer trust inconsistencies.

P1Attribution is dominated by direct

GA4 lists 90% direct traffic, low Google organic sessions, and small social and referral counts.

Action: apply consistent UTMs to social, outreach, email, portfolio links and campaigns. Document source and medium conventions. Investigate referrer loss and form and WhatsApp journeys.

Measure: more attributable sessions and leads. Lower "unknown/direct" share where campaign tags are controllable.

P1Traffic quality needs segmentation

Average engagement is 23 seconds, but sources and geography may include irrelevant or low-quality traffic.

Action: segment engagement by source, landing page, hostname, device, country and city, and event behaviour before excluding anything. Confirm internal traffic filters and bot and security signals.

Measure: qualified engaged sessions. Lead conversion by source. Transparent, defensible traffic-quality filters.

P1Content velocity has stopped

Impressions fell from 135 in July to 27 in late October. No new content, no internal linking refresh.

Action: establish a content cadence tied to keyword clusters. Even a stable site loses relevance without one.

Measure: non-brand impressions trend. New indexed pages. Returning visitor rate.

P1High bounce on the resume page

80.7% bounce on the resume page. People land and leave without clicking anything else. Same pattern as NMKCA's ranking PDF.

Action: add onward paths from the resume page — case studies, contact, specific service pages. Make the next step obvious.

Measure: resume-page exit rate. Portfolio-to-contact click-through. Qualified briefs.
