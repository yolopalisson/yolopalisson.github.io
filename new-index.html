NMK Chartered Accountants

Brand & Website · 2026

A professional website redesign for a financial services firm, modernising their digital presence to establish trust, clarify services, and strengthen client acquisition.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    As a financial services firm, first impressions are critical. The website needed to convey professionalism, accuracy, and reliability immediately. The challenge was clarifying a complex service portfolio (accounting, auditing, tax, payroll, advisory), improving lead generation pathways, and creating a contemporary visual identity appropriate for professional services.

  2. 02

    Approach

    Our approach involved three phases: discovery and positioning to define clear messaging around financial clarity and compliance; information architecture restructuring so visitors quickly find services, expertise, and contact information; and modern visual design with improved typography, hierarchy, and responsive optimisation. We integrated conversion-focused elements including clear contact pathways, service highlights, and strategic calls-to-action throughout.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    The completed website gives NMK a stronger digital foundation aligned with its reputation as a trusted financial services partner. The new design better reflects the firms expertise, services are now clearly communicated, usability improved across devices, and simplified navigation with clearer CTAs strengthens lead generation potential. The website provides a scalable platform for future growth and digital initiatives.

NMK Chartered Accountants is a South African accounting and advisory firm specialising in auditing, tax, financial management, and business advisory. The previous digital presence did not fully communicate the firms breadth of services or value proposition.

We partnered with NMK to create a professional website that reflects credibility, expertise, and a client-focused approach while improving how prospective clients discover and engage with the business.

Expert17

Website · 2025

A clean, professional company website for Expert17 — built in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to establish digital credibility.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    Companies in Expert17’s sector are judged heavily by their web presence. A site that feels outdated undermines confidence before a conversation starts.

  2. 02

    Approach

    Three phases: discovery (understanding the business and its competitive context), design (high-fidelity Figma mockups iterated with the client), and development (clean, semantic HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no framework dependencies). Content architecture focused on three journeys: learning about the company, understanding services, and making contact.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    Expert17 launched its site and began using it actively in business development. The project was completed on schedule, however another redesign is in progress.

Expert17 required a company website that presented their business clearly and professionally, serving as a credible digital destination for potential clients conducting due diligence.

Green Lumber Holdings

Website · 2024

A complete website overhaul, modernising Green Lumber Holdings’ digital presence while preserving brand equity and sharpening the commercial message.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    Website rebrands risk alienating existing customers if too aggressive, and failing to attract new audiences if too conservative. Retaining what worked while replacing what didn’t was a delicate balance.

  2. 02

    Approach

    A content audit identified high-value pages from analytics data. The visual design was evolved, not replaced: colour palette retained but refined, and a new typography system introduced hierarchy and polish. Structured data markup was added throughout. Rebuild in standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript significantly improved load performance.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    Page load times improved substantially. The client reported a marked increase in professional enquiries. Green Lumber Holdings have since become a retained client.

Green Lumber Holdings had an existing website that no longer reflected the growth and professionalism of the business. The rebrand required careful stakeholder management while delivering a meaningfully more capable digital presence.

Loan Eligibility Simulator

Web Application · 2026

Property portfolio site. Listing management, photography integration, a content model built for scale.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    The portfolio was growing. Every new property required manual developer work to add.

  2. 02

    Approach

    Content model built for growth. Listings managed from a single CMS interface, photography integrated end to end.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    New properties added in under five minutes by the client team. Portfolio grew 3× without a rebuild.

A property portfolio that grows is a content problem disguised as a design problem. LM Holdings was adding a new listing every few weeks, and every one was a small rebuild — new page, new hero image crop, new gallery layout, new PDF brochure.

We built the site around a listing model rather than around a page. Every property is structured data with its own images, features, floor plans, and brochure. Adding a listing is filling in a form. The site renders itself.

05

BitFit

Product Interface · 2024

Product interface for a fitness tracking app. Design system and component library. Built for the product team, documented for the engineering team.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    The product team was shipping inconsistent screens. No design system in place, no shared components.

  2. 02

    Approach

    Audit, then a new design system. Components built and documented for the engineering team, not just handed off as Figma.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    Ship velocity up. Inconsistencies eliminated. Onboarding docs reused as the team grows.

BitFit's product team had six engineers and no shared vocabulary. Every screen was a small negotiation between the designer's Figma and whatever the developer remembered from the last one. Nothing was wrong. Everything was slightly different.

We started with an audit — every screen, side by side. Then a new design system, not as documentation but as a working component library the engineers could use directly. Every component has states, tokens, and usage rules. It shipped as code, not just as artboards.

Harbourline Landing Page

Website · Landing Page · 2026

Landing page for a maritime services company. Lead generation, service information, and contact forms. Currently in build.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    Complex service offerings and a diverse target audience required a clear value proposition and easy navigation.

  2. 02

    Approach

    One commerce platform with subscription, wholesale, and POS all writing to the same customer database. Two wholesale portals, one admin.

Outpost started as three businesses wearing one logo. Their subscription coffee, wholesale accounts, and two café locations each had their own checkout, their own customer records, and their own spreadsheets. A subscriber picking up coffee in-store was a stranger at the counter.

The build consolidates everything into a single commerce platform. Subscriptions, wholesale ordering, and café POS all write to the same customer database. Trade customers get their own portal. Walk-in sales debit the same loyalty balance as online. Currently in build with launch scheduled for Q3 2026.
