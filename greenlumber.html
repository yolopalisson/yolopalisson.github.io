Who We Are

We are driven by the belief that plastic waste can be transformed into valuable products that offer durable, practical and environmentally responsible alternatives to traditional materials.

Green Lumber Holdings operates through two subsidiaries:

  • Green Lumber Manufacturing (Pty) LtdResponsible for manufacturing a range of products using recycled plastic, offering durable and low-maintenance solutions for commercial, industrial and outdoor environments.
  • Green Lumber Leasing (Pty) LtdProvides security guard leasing solutions, making our products accessible to businesses through flexible rental arrangements.

Together, the two divisions support our commitment to sustainability, quality, innovation and customer-focused service.

Why Choose Green Lumber?

100% Recycled Plastic

Customer-Focused

Our Vision

To become Africa's number one manufacturer of eco-friendly products, championing environmental innovation and social impact through local job creation.

Our Mission

To be the leading provider of sustainable green products by recycling plastic waste into customised, environmentally friendly products for personal and workplace environments, while delivering customer-centric service, professionalism and the highest industry standards.
