Core Web Vitals thresholds used for evaluation

At the 75th percentile, "good" targets are LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms and CLS ≤ 0.1. These are evaluation targets. The measured values for Cybit and NMKCA (CLS ≈ 0.9) sit outside the "good" band by a factor of nine.

Google's guidance on AI search

Google's own guidance says the established SEO fundamentals remain relevant for AI search features, and that there are no additional technical requirements or special AI-specific schema needed for eligibility. The recommendations here therefore focus on crawlability, helpful content, clear text, internal linking, evidence, and structured data that accurately matches visible content. [S5]

Pressure touchpoints and proposed improvements

P0CLS is a conversion leak

A CLS of ~0.9 on desktop, worse on mobile, suppresses form completion and webinar registration. Every layout shift is a moment where a prospect loses their place mid-form or mid-CTA. On a site whose commercial model runs on webinar sign-ups and campaign enquiries, that friction compounds into lost pipeline.

Fix: trace the shift source in the PageSpeed filmstrip. Reserve space for HubSpot forms and banners. Set explicit dimensions on hero media. Defer non-critical third-party tags (LeadForensics, Demandbase, browser speed scripts) until after interaction.

Measure: CLS ≤ 0.1. Form completion rate. Webinar registration rate. Field data (CrUX) once sample size allows.

P0Measurement to CRM

No Cybit GA4, GSC or CRM baseline was available for this audit. This is a data gap, not proof that tracking is absent. But it means the current form-to-pipeline path cannot be verified from outside.

Fix: audit GA4 events, consent state, HubSpot and CRM properties, Lead Forensics, Sales Navigator and webinar hand-offs. Map every form, assessment booking, phone and email click, gated asset and webinar registration.

Measure: event QA pass rate. Lead-source completeness. Form-submit-to-CRM match rate. Visitor-to-lead conversion. Marketing-sourced and influenced pipeline.

P1Third-party script governance

Four tracking platforms (HubSpot, LeadForensics, Demandbase, browser speed) load on the homepage. Each competes for main-thread time and each contributes to layout shift unless explicitly controlled.

Fix: load on interaction or after consent. Set explicit container dimensions. Audit which tags fire before the first paint.

Measure: TBT, INP, consent rate, and CLS stability during releases.

P1Landing page conversion architecture

Service pages are content-rich, but buyers arriving with a specific problem may not find a crisp next step or relevant proof quickly enough.

Experiment: for each priority page, test a clear primary CTA, a lower-commitment secondary CTA, one relevant case study or outcome, scope/fit qualifiers, and a concise "what happens next" section.

Measure: CTA click-to-submit. Form completion. Assessment booking rate. Qualified-lead rate by page.

P1Information architecture

Multiple corporate and content destinations (service pages, technologies, blog, Press Centre, Engage Hub, On Demand, Store). That breadth is an asset but can complicate choice if paths are not clearly tiered.

Fix: use GA4 path exploration and heatmaps to confirm whether target buyers find the right service route. Add prominent "I need to…" pathways and contextual links between problem, service, case study and CTA.

Measure: path progression. Service-to-case-study CTR. Exits at key pages. Contact-page assist rate.

P1AI visibility

The role explicitly expects visibility in ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews. No AI visibility baseline exists for Cybit.

Fix: create a repeatable prompt set by buyer, vertical and solution. Record whether Cybit is mentioned or cited, which competitors appear, which source pages are cited and what content gaps recur.

Measure: share of tracked prompts with a correct mention. Citation frequency. Cited page set. AI-assisted visits and leads.

P1Non-brand discoverability

No Cybit Search Console export or ranking data was available. Query gaps cannot be quantified without it.

Fix: build a baseline around problem and solution searches for CIO, IT, CISO and data leaders. Map each intent cluster to one strong landing page and useful supporting content.

Measure: non-brand clicks and impressions. Qualified organic leads. Priority-query coverage. Assisted pipeline.

P2Structured data depth

Yoast Premium outputs a basic schema graph, but Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Review markup on service and campaign pages is likely incomplete. For a B2B technology firm, this is unclaimed territory.

Fix: deploy Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Review schema where eligible. Validate against visible content before publishing.

Measure: rich result impressions. CTR on pages with structured data.

The best next move is optimisation, not a wholesale redesign. The content foundation is strong and the conversion infrastructure exists. Use behavioural evidence to determine which entry pages fail to progress visitors, and test small changes against a control. Confirm templates, metadata, indexability, schema, internal linking and mobile behaviour with a crawl audit before marking anything as a defect.

Pressure touchpoints and recommended fixes

P0Suspicious URL patterns

Search Console contains paths that do not fit an accounting website: "?goods/…", "?jp/…", "/outlet/…" and "/ctg/search/similarImageSearchResultView/".

Action: inspect exact URLs in a browser and Search Console URL Inspection. Check response, canonical, indexability, sitemap, CMS records, plugins and users, and server/security logs. If confirmed invalid, clean up the source and return appropriate 404, 410 or redirects. Do not mass-redirect unrelated junk without validation.

Measure: no unapproved spam URLs in the index. Clean sitemap. Stable indexed-page count. No recurring suspicious URL patterns.

P0CLS is the same leak as Cybit

Desktop CLS is around 0.9. Mobile is worse. The site is unstable on load, which affects both engagement and search quality signals. WordPress with unmanaged script loading is a common cause.

Action: trace the shift source, reserve space for dynamic elements, defer non-critical scripts, set explicit media dimensions.

Measure: CLS ≤ 0.1. Engagement time. Contact form completion.

P0Zero-value trust counters

Homepage text extraction shows multiple trust counters at 0, while the page copy separately states 24+ years of industry experience. Crawlers and assistive technology may see zeroes where the browser eventually shows numbers.

Action: check the rendered page, count-up animation and server-rendered text. Make key proof points meaningful without JavaScript, and ensure counts are accurate.

Measure: consistent counters across browser, accessibility tree and crawler extraction. No zero-value trust metrics.

P1Legacy PDF ranking

"NMK-Company-Profile-2017-emailer.pdf": 676 impressions, 14 clicks, 2.07% CTR, position 6.45. A nine-year-old PDF is the second-best performing asset on the site.

Action: confirm whether the profile is current. Replace with a current branded PDF and accurate metadata. Update internal links and decide whether the legacy URL should be retained, redirected or retired based on link equity and user need.

Measure: current asset receives clicks and downloads. Updated dates, service information and contact details. No stale company claims.

P1Homepage concentration

The homepage is the main indexed page (223 clicks, 5,188 impressions, 4.3% CTR, position 8.75). Service query intent is dispersed.

Action: map each high-value service to a dedicated page with descriptive title and H1, clear scope, South African context, proof, FAQs and a single primary CTA. Link service, industry, about, leadership and contact pages logically.

Measure: non-brand clicks. High-intent landing-page visits. Contact and consultation conversion by service.

P1Mobile CTR gap

Mobile has lower CTR than desktop (3.82% versus 5.36%) even though average position is stronger.

Action: check mobile SERP appearance and run a device-specific journey audit covering viewport, speed, menu, text scale, tap targets, contact and WhatsApp link, and form friction. Segment by query before attributing cause.

Measure: mobile CTR and mobile lead conversion by landing page. Fewer form drop-offs.

P2No meaningful rich-result signal

Search appearance rows show one impression each for review and product snippets across six months.

Action: validate structured data against visible content — Organization, AccountingService or LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList, Person where relevant. Use FAQ content to answer users, not to chase guaranteed rich snippets.

Measure: valid structured data where eligible. Stronger SERP clarity. Qualified organic traffic.

Core service pages

External audit, internal audit, independent reviews, audit committee support, tax compliance, payroll and PAYE, annual financial statements, management accounts, bookkeeping, company secretarial, business advisory.
